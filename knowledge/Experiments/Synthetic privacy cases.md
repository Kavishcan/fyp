# Synthetic privacy cases

Fictional sensitive queries and planted identifiers make leakage measurable without introducing real private patient information. Earlier privacy/attack harnesses include 200 privacy and sixty malicious cases; de-identification fixtures are additional.

Tests validate the specific strings, endpoints and modes exercised. They do not certify clinical privacy or transfer older A3 outcomes to blind mode automatically.
