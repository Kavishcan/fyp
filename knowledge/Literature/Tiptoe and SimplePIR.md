# Tiptoe and SimplePIR

Primary paper link for Tiptoe: [Private Web Search with Tiptoe](https://doi.org/10.1145/3600006.3613134).

Private search/PIR demonstrates established private-access systems and offline/online cost trade-offs. SimplePIR is a different work; do not conflate it with Tiptoe or claim this prototype implements either.
