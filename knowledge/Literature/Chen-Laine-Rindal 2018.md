# Chen-Laine-Rindal 2018

Primary author record: [Label-PSI from Fully Homomorphic Encryption with Malicious Security](https://ladnir.github.io/publications/2018/10/06/labeled-psi.html).

Hao Chen, Kim Laine and Peter Rindal, CCS 2018; ePrint 2018/787. Labelled PSI provides a relevant conceptual precedent.

This paper uses FHE and a specified malicious-security protocol. Our group-OPRF cluster tables are not that protocol, and do not inherit its proof.
