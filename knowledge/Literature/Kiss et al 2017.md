# Kiss et al 2017

Primary source: [Private Set Intersection for Unequal Set Sizes with Mobile Applications](https://www.encrypto.cs.tu-darmstadt.de/media/encrypto/encrypto_papers/KLSAP17.pdf); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/popets-2017-0044).

Authors: Agnes Kiss, Jian Liu, Thomas Schneider, N. Asokan and Benny Pinkas. This work studies unequal-set PSI and setup/online trade-offs.

It motivates precomputation; the prototype should not be described as an exact implementation of one of its protocols without a detailed mapping.
