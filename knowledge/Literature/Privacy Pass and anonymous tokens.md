# Privacy Pass and anonymous tokens

Related standardised anonymous authorisation work. Verify the exact standard/profile and issuer/attester/redemption model before choosing an implementation.

Unlinkable role tokens could reduce credential identity exposure, but stable rate limiting and accountability complicate the design. The present HMAC credentials are not Privacy Pass.
