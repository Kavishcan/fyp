# Source attack

The attacker predicts the genuinely relevant source behind covers. Repeated random-decoy contacts can expose the persistent real source.

Blind all-node contact reaches source chance .125 over one-five snippets in the eight-node session experiment. This pertains to contact-set observations; node-profile knowledge, identity and timing are not covered.
