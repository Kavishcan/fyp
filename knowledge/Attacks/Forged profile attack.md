# Forged profile attack

A source can sign and publish misleading centroids or poisoned evidence to attract selection and affect answers. Signature authenticity is not truthfulness.

Earlier sixty-case attacks and partial trust/rerank mitigations apply to their measured modes. The current blind cosine planner has no transferred trust defence. It also contacts every node, so define the attack target as useful-cluster selection/opening/citation, not contact alone.
