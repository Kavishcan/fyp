# Cell churn intersection (audit, docs/49)

[[Anonymity cells]] were rebuilt from the registry per query; one join moved 8 of 16 nodes to new cells; intersecting before/after cells shrank an anonymity set to 1. Not fixed — cells need epoch-fixed, join-stable construction; [[Blind unlock]] has no cells to churn.
