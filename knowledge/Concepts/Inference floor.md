# Inference floor

The accuracy an attacker gets with no signal: the **majority-class rate** of the labels (not 1/k on skewed labels). [[PMC-Patients]] floors: 0.239 (k-means), 0.210 (Dirichlet), 0.141 (random) — see [[Hospital splits]]. [[Blind unlock]] sits exactly at the floor on all three.
