# Honest-but-curious

A party that follows the protocol but tries to learn from what it sees. The hospitals' default model. [[Formal leakage]] claim 1 (question hiding) also holds against malicious and colluding hospitals because their view is independent of the question; integrity against malicious hospitals is not claimed ([[Forged profile attack]], [[Verifiable OPRF]]).
