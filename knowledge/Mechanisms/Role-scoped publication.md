# Role-scoped publication

The public profile (centroids, topics, doc count) is built from public documents only; restricted centroids come from `get_restricted_centroids(auth)`, allow-list checked and Ed25519-signed, verified fail-closed by the device. Clinical centroids visible 1.000 → 0.000 to unpermitted roles. Part of [[Role-based access]].
