# Cross-node evidence rerank

The device re-scores returned evidence and limits the passages supplied to generation. Older planted-passage experiments reduce attacker citation from 1.000 to .067 at top-two evidence, while the attacker may still be selected.

This is not cryptographic integrity and not a demonstrated blind-mode profile-hijack defence. Validate transfer to cached blind tables and realistic poisoning.
