# Dummy points

A real probe is r·H(c) with r uniform — a uniform element of the prime-order group. A dummy is r·G with r uniform — the same distribution (fixed-base multiplication, ~7× cheaper). A hospital cannot tell them apart, so it cannot tell whether it was relevant. Used by [[Blind unlock]] and [[Cover traffic]]. Proof: [[Formal leakage]] claim 1.
