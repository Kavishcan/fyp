# RBAC results

In tested role-gated fixtures, access matches the configured role/collection matrix and lying about a role gains no additional access. Mixed public/restricted fixtures show clinical centroid visibility dropping from 1.000 to .000 for unpermitted roles.

Do not generalise to restricted-only nodes: public_view fallback exposes their structure. Signature enforcement also depends on key provisioning and registry policy. Authorised clients can retain opened data.
